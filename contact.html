FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Do you only shoot in the DMV?

Yes. DC, PG County, and Northern Virginia only. This isn't a national operation — the local focus is intentional. I know the venues, the culture, and the scene here. That familiarity shows in the work.

How fast do I get my photos?

Same-week delivery on everything. For events, you get a preview set the same night. The full edited gallery comes within 48 hours. No waiting two weeks while the hype dies.

Everything really shot on a phone?

100%. That's the entire point. Mobile photos live on the same device your guests are scrolling on. They translate perfectly to Instagram, TikTok, and Reels without any compression or reformatting gymnastics.

What's the 2016 aesthetic exactly?

Flash photography, motion blur, candid moments, real color. Before everything got desaturated and "cinematic." Photos that feel like a memory, not a product shoot. Vine-era energy in a 2025 context.

Can I book multiple dates?

Yes — and the Monthly Subscription ($500–1,500/mo) is built exactly for that. Consistent content across 1–2 shoots per month, cohesive aesthetic maintained, priority scheduling all season.

Do I own the photos?

You receive fully edited files you can post anywhere. Blacctography retains the right to use images in the portfolio and social media — which is standard practice and also free marketing for your event.
