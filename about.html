The Story

Blacctography was built on a simple idea: the best camera is the one that actually captures the moment. Not the one set up on a tripod across the room. Not the one that takes 45 minutes to set up before the party starts. The one in your pocket — pointed at the right thing at the right time.

The DMV has always had a distinct energy. The parties hit different here. The culture runs deep. And for too long, that energy was being documented by photographers who were technically skilled but culturally disconnected. Blacctography is the correction.

Rooted in the 2016 era — when flash photos actually looked like flash photos, when motion blur meant something, when candid meant everyone was having too much fun to notice the camera — this is documentation that feels like it was made by someone who was there. Because it was.

The Approach

HOW I
WORK.

📱
Phone Only

Everything is shot on a mobile phone. That's not a limitation — it's the entire point. Mobile photos look like the moment. They live on the same device your guests are scrolling on. They translate perfectly to every platform.

⚡
Flash First

The flash is the signature. That overexposed, flat-lit, mid-laugh energy from 2016 isn't an accident — it's a deliberate aesthetic choice that makes every photo feel like a real memory instead of a posed catalog shot.

🎯
Candid Always

No poses. No "okay, everyone look at the camera." I'm looking for the moments between the moments — laughing mid-sentence, motion blur on a dance floor, two people in a real conversation. That's the content that gets saved and shared.

🌴
2016 Aesthetic

Before everything got desaturated and "cinematic," photos had color, grain, and personality. That era had a distinct visual culture that hit different — and that's the reference point for every shoot.

⚡
Fast Delivery

Same-week turnaround on everything. Events get previews the same night. Your content is in your hands while the hype is still alive — not two weeks later when the moment is dead.

🗺️
DMV Only

This isn't a national operation. DC, PG County, NoVA — this is the market, the culture, the scene I know. That familiarity shows in every photo.
